Place your image assets here. Required filenames (rename your files to match):

  cover.jpeg              ← used full-bleed on page 1
  work-main.jpeg          ← main view of the work, page 4
  work-closeup.jpeg       ← detail or alternate angle of the same work, page 5
  artist-portrait.png     ← page 2 biography portrait

Format:
- sRGB color space (not Adobe RGB, not CMYK)
- JPEG quality 90 for photographs; PNG for line-art or precise edges
- ~300 dpi at output size (2000–3000 px on the long edge is plenty)

For 2D works (painting, drawing, collage), detour the background to pure white
before saving. For sculpture / 3D, keep the original studio background.
